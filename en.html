Golden Visa Residency

According to the latest changes implemented in 2026, there are three options for obtaining the Greek Golden Visa through property purchase.

1‑ Golden Visa with a threshold of €800,000: In densely populated areas with high demand and populations above 3,100—such as Athens, Thessaloniki, and islands like Santorini and Mykonos—the minimum investment is €800,000 (with a minimum residential property size of 120 m²).

2‑ Golden Visa with a threshold of €400,000: In less densely populated areas and islands with fewer than 3,100 residents, the minimum investment is €400,000 (with a minimum residential property size of 120 m²).

3‑ Golden Visa with a threshold of €250,000: In all regions of Greece, if you purchase commercial, office, or industrial properties that have been converted into residential use, you can obtain the Greek Golden Visa with a minimum investment of €250,000 without any restrictions.

The Greek Golden Visa is granted to non‑EU nationals who invest €250,000 in real estate or other eligible investment routes in Greece. By obtaining the Greek Golden Visa, investors and their families (parents, spouse, children, and spouse’s parents) receive a renewable 5‑year residency permit. If the investor keeps ownership of the property for 7 years (the property is not sold), they may apply for Greek citizenship through naturalization, provided they reside in Greece for at least 183 days per year. Investors must also meet additional requirements, including proving integration into Greek society and passing a language exam. According to the CEFR framework, applicants must achieve at least level B1 in the Greek language exam, known as the Greek citizenship exam.

Benefits of the Greek Golden Visa

The Greek Golden Visa offers many advantages for applicants and their families, including the following key benefits:

Access to Schengen countries: One of the greatest benefits of the Greek Golden Visa is visa‑free travel to all Schengen member states. This includes 27 European countries, allowing Golden Visa holders to travel easily for business or leisure.

Residence in Greece: With the Golden Visa, applicants and their families can legally reside in Greece and enjoy its natural beauty, rich culture, and desirable lifestyle.

Education and healthcare: Golden Visa holders and their families can access Greece’s educational and healthcare systems, including reputable schools, universities, and high‑quality medical services.

Investment opportunities: Residency in Greece allows applicants to benefit from investment and business opportunities in one of Europe’s growing economies. Greece’s strategic location and diverse markets offer many investment possibilities.

Lower cost of living: The cost of living in Greece is lower compared to many other European countries, allowing Golden Visa holders to enjoy a higher quality of life at more reasonable expenses.

Eligibility for citizenship: After 7 years of legal residency in Greece, Golden Visa holders may apply for Greek citizenship.

Stability and security: As an EU member state, Greece offers political and economic stability, ensuring a safe and secure environment for Golden Visa holders.

Obtaining a Greek tax number (AFM): To complete any official transaction in Greece, obtaining a tax number is mandatory. This number is issued by the Greek tax authority and is usually obtained through an authorized lawyer.

Required Documents:

• Official power of attorney

• Completed tax forms

The investment transfer must be done through the official banking system. Therefore, opening a bank account in one of the Greek banks is necessary. At this stage:

• Source of Funds is reviewed

Property selection and legal due diligence

After selecting the property, your lawyer reviews the title deed, any potential debts, property usage, and construction permits. This stage is crucial because any legal issue can disrupt the residency process.

Items reviewed include:

• Property registration status

• Tax liabilities

• Building permits and completion certificates

• Absence of legal restrictions

Signing the contract and official transfer of funds

After final approval, the purchase contract is signed at the Greek notary office. The funds are transferred from the buyer’s account to the seller, and the official deed is registered under the investor’s name. At this stage:

• Property transfer tax is paid

• Notary fees are settled

• The official deed is issued

Submitting the residency application (Biometrics) After completing the purchase, the residency file is submitted to the Greek immigration office. This stage includes:

• Submission of all required documents

• Fingerprint registration (biometrics)

• Receiving the temporary residency receipt

With this receipt, free travel within the Schengen area is allowed. Issuance of the 5‑year residency card After final review of the file, the Golden Visa residency card is issued. This card is typically valid for 5 years and can be renewed as long as the investment is maintained.

Important note: To renew the Greek Golden Visa, there is no minimum stay requirement; the only condition is maintaining the investment.

Here are some of the most important additional costs:

Property purchase tax:

When buying property in Greece, buyers must pay the property transfer tax. This tax is usually around 7% of the purchase price. In addition to the purchase tax, there may be additional taxes such as annual property tax (IMT), depending on the value of the property.

Health insurance costs:

Golden Visa applicants and their family members must have valid health insurance for residency in Greece. The cost of private health insurance depends on coverage type, age, and health condition. Health insurance is typically calculated annually and must be purchased separately for each individual.

Lawyer and immigration consultant fees:

Legal and advisory services from specialized lawyers and consultants are required during the Golden Visa process. These fees vary depending on the complexity of the case. Lawyer fees may range from €2,000 to €10,000 depending on the level of assistance needed.

Administrative and official documentation fees:

During the Golden Visa process, costs for document translation, registration fees, and required certifications may apply. These costs vary depending on the number of documents and the need for translation and approval by Greek authorities.
